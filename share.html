English invitation for educators and international students

We invite school and university students, parents, teachers, and academic counselors in the UAE to complete a short anonymous survey about exam preparation, time management, question banks, mock exams, and digital learning tools. It takes 3–5 minutes. We do not ask for names, phone numbers, email addresses, or school names. Participation is voluntary, and results will be reported in aggregate with the sample size, method, and limitations. Survey link: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd_RzbwFVqtW2eUFFxmnsWznP2kivVjgP1CGAs_ONG-AMnzwQ/viewform
